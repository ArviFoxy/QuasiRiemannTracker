\begin{abstract}
We refine the affine local-moment argument to the exact boundary
$8748673/10000000=0.8748673$. The change allocates the available LOW saving
unequally between auxiliary fees and retained saving, enlarging the permitted
capacity defect to $1/241$. The amplifier, source-volume cap, and final
analytic savings are retained. Exact row certificates then give the revised
endpoint. The full analytic argument builds on Kari Roegnvaldsson's affine
capacity, nielstron's local $22/5$ proof, and their mathematical predecessors.
\end{abstract}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
For every positive integer $q$, every complex Dirichlet character $\chi$
modulo $q$, and every $s\in\C$ satisfying
\[
 \Re s>\frac{8748673}{10000000},\qquad
 \neg(\chi=1\ \text{and}\ s=1),
\]
the canonical analytically continued Dirichlet $L$-function $L(\chi,s)$ is nonzero.
\end{theorem}

\section{The saving available to the actual source}
The affine local argument retains two separate geometric requirements:
\[
 n_1+n_2+z\le\frac{881}{1000},\qquad
 n_1+n_2+\frac92z\le1+g.
\]
Here $z$ is the local prime length and $g$ bounds the old-capacity defect.
The amplifier remains $a=3/500$. The raw LOW reserve supplied by the
incumbent ledger is
\[
 r_{\rm raw}(g)=\frac3{500}-\frac{67}{450}
                  \left(g+6\frac3{500}\right).
\]
This is a reserve for the original coefficient source, before the separately
recorded auxiliary losses. At $g=1/241$,
\[
 r_{\rm raw}(1/241)=\frac{301}{13556250}>\frac1{46000}.
\]
Use the following unequal split:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Raw reserve & Paid auxiliary fees & Retained LOW saving\\
\midrule
$1/46000$ & $1/2000000$ & $1/48000$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
It is valid because
\[
 \frac1{46000}-\frac1{2000000}-\frac1{48000}
 =\frac7{17250000}>0.
\]
Thus, whenever the actual padded-source ledger has fee
$\mathrm{pad}+\mathrm{loss}+(67/450)\,\mathrm{geometryLoss}\le1/2000000$,
its exponent is at most the nominal target minus $1/48000$.
The fee bound and retained saving are distinct quantities in the source
inequality; there is no requirement to divide the raw reserve into equal halves.

The common tiny loss is chosen to be $\eta=10^{-8}$. The original fixed
profile and logarithmic losses can be made this small by enlarging the common
height threshold. After eleven such fees, the retained saving still exceeds
the unchanged final LOW target:
\[
 \frac1{48000}-11\eta-\frac1{50000}
 =\frac{217}{300000000}>0.
\]
For the extracted errors, the same annular amplifier gives prime length
$\lambda\ge9/2000$. The three raw reserves remain at least $1/1250$:
\[
 \lambda-\frac{g+9/500}{6},\qquad
 6\lambda-\frac{g+9/500+6\lambda}{6},\qquad
 7\lambda-\frac{g+9/500+6\lambda}{6}\ \ge\ \frac1{1250}.
\]
The first, tightest expression is $1169/1446000>1/1250$ at the endpoint.
This retains the downstream $1/2000$, $1/2500$, and final $1/10000$ savings.

\section{Capacity and coefficient transport}
For the same bare physical window $419/1000\le n\le422/1000$, define
\[
 b_g(n)=\frac{1+g-2n}{9/2},\qquad
 c_g(n,t)=\max\left\{0,\frac{1+g-2(n+t)}{9/2}\right\}.
\]
The local branch is used only when $0\le t\le3/4000$. With $g=1/241$,
the original volume gate follows from the exact worst-case bound
\[
 2(n+t)+c_g(n,t)
 \le\frac{3820717}{4338000}<\frac{881}{1000}.
\]
Also $c_g(n,t)\le20021/542250<37/1000$ and $n+t<423/1000$,
so the original physical window remains valid. The two diagonal constraints
for independent plain heights are carried through separately.

Past $t=3/4000$, use the old capacity
$c_0(n,t)=\max\{0,(1-2(n+t))/(9/2)\}$.
If $c(n,t)$ denotes the resulting piecewise capacity, then
\[
 b_g(n)-c(n,t)\le2t.
\]
On the local branch this follows directly from coefficient dilation. On the
old branch it follows from $g\le7t$, since $g=1/241<21/4000$.
For the actual scalar parameters $0\le q\le\delta/2$, this gives the repayment
\[
 \frac{q\,b_g(n)}2\le\frac{\delta t}{2}+\frac{q\,c(n,t)}2.
\]
Consequently the wider capacity is paid by the original weighted coefficient
mass, including free shifts and the explicitly charged profile padding.

\section{Exact geometry and the complete row saving}
The linked parameters are
\begin{align*}

\end{align*}
They satisfy $\theta=11/12-\ell/4$ and $\kappa_0=2\theta-1$.
Set $j=h-1/1000$ and $\zeta=10^{-10}$.
For $0\le\delta\le3/4$ and $0\le p\le1/2$, put
\[
 V=5/6-\delta,\quad D=3-p-\frac{2p}{3\kappa_0},\quad
 B=2(1-p)\left(1-\frac p{3\kappa_0}\right),\quad J=VD+\delta B>0.
\]
The literal row-count exponent is
$R_{\kappa_0}=1-\delta+\delta BV/(2J)$.
Above the junction, the normalized affine-source exponent is
\begin{align*}
 E_g(d,\delta,p)
 &=\frac12+\frac{13h}{75}-\frac y2-\frac\ell2+\frac{33d}{50}
 +\delta\left(\frac12-\frac{3d}{4}\right)\\
 &\quad+\delta p\left(\ell-\frac{2y-d+gd}{9}\right)
 +\frac{3d\delta BV}{8J}.
\end{align*}
The added $gd$ term is the retained affine defect allowance, expressed in
the actual source ledger. Below the junction the incumbent $6\kappa$ source
estimate is retained, with its original breakpoint at $d=y$.

Multiplying $\theta-r-E_g$ by $8J>0$ and putting $u=1/2-p$ gives
$Q_0(\delta)+uQ_1(\delta)+u^2Q_2(\delta)+u^3Q_3(\delta)$.
The coefficients are regenerated exactly. For $i=0,1$, writing
$Q_i(t)=a_it^2+b_it+c_i$, one checks $a_i>0$ and $4a_ic_i-b_i^2\ge0$.
Then $4a_iQ_i(t)=(2a_it+b_i)^2+(4a_ic_i-b_i^2)\ge0$.
The remaining two quadratics have nonnegative coefficients on $t\ge0$.
The top row's first discriminant gap is, exactly,
\[
 \frac{3409524688575287555695910111111}
 {301218586396153715851256250000000000}>0.
\]
The same regenerated certificate method proves reserves $1/2000000$ at
$d=h$, $1/10000$ at $d=j$, and $1/100$ at $d=1/2$.
The full coefficient tables and exact identities accompany the source.

Interpolation covers $1/200\le d\le h+\zeta$. The top slope is at most $1$,

\section{The unconditional endpoint}
The local moment and the geometric reserve checks are applied to the
original source packet. The complete analytic proof retains coherent
amplification, joint row reconstruction, cancellation in the first-kernel
errors, coprimality, ramified labels, finite Euler deletions, independent
heights, and the low, exceptional, and large-height estimates.
The actual padded-source consumers use the asymmetric split in Section~1.
The common auxiliary losses and height are chosen before the target character.

The literal physical exponent is identified with its scalar ledger and
actual normalization. Equation~\eqref{eq:feedback} supplies a positive saving;
integration and normalization prove the required analytic source bound.
The same normalized probe appears in LOW and HIGH. The retained analytic
correction and principal-term estimates then complete the continuation
argument, bounding the actual Hecke-zero supremum by $\theta$.
Finally, the proved Hecke-to-Dirichlet identity yields every modulus and
character in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, with the sole principal pole excluded.
The Lean endpoint is the canonical theorem \code{Candidate.bound}.
