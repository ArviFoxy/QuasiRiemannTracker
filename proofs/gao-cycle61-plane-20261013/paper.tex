\begin{abstract}
We specialize Hailey Collet's submitted Cycle61 construction to the exact
boundary $\theta=4374299/5000000=0.8748598$. The refinement changes the
LOW/HIGH density split and solves two reserve-cancellation equations together.
The resulting rational parameters retain positive main, error and exceptional

connect those source estimates to the endpoint. This note explains
the changed mathematics; the complete inherited analytic assembly accompanies
it in 194 Lean modules. Verification status: \verificationstatus.

\section{Target statement and the role of the refinement}
We prove the following assertion. For every positive
integer $m$, every complex Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $m$, and every
$s\in\C$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
 \Re s>\theta,\qquad \neg(\chi=1\text{ and }s=1)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad L(\chi,s)\ne0.
\end{equation}
The principal pole is the only exception in the submitted statement. The
entry point is \texttt{Candidate.bound}; the source also includes the Hecke
and total-zeta comparison statements used by the tracker.

The predecessor is Collet's Cycle61 source at the immutable revision
\texttt{fb6d4fd}, submitted in PR~42 with boundary $0.874861$.
The reduction is exactly $3/2500000=0.0000012$.
That submitted source supplies the physical row reconstruction, common probe,
uniform analytic main/error estimates and final zero-exclusion argument.
The present contribution is a linked parameter choice and its exact
certificates, propagated through the actual consumers of the inherited proof.

\section{A density split that balances both main estimates}
Let $q$ denote the LOW/HIGH cutoff, not the modulus in \eqref{eq:target}.
On the dictionary range used here, $13/20\le\delta\le3/4$
(equivalently $33/40\le\sigma\le7/8$), the inherited unmarked density exponent is
\[
 \rho(\delta)=1-\frac{10\delta}{3(5-2\delta)},\qquad
 q=\frac{67}{100},\qquad \rho_0=\rho(q)=\frac{214}{549}.
\]
The actual LOW/HIGH partition uses the ordinary zero-real cutoff
$\sigma_0=(1+q)/2=167/200=0.835$. Thus $\delta=2\sigma-1\le q$ on the LOW side
and $\delta\ge q$ on the HIGH side.
Write $\epsilon=10^{-5}$, $\alpha=1/400000$ and
$b_j=17/500+j\epsilon$ for $1\le j\le600$.
The source uses three linked quantities:
\[
 g(b)=B-1+(9/2-\lambda)b,\quad
 a(b)=K g(b)+\alpha,\quad
 A(b)=B-(\lambda-1)(b-\epsilon).
\]
Here $g$ is the capacity defect, $a$ the amplification allowance, and $A$ the
main source-volume exponent. They must be chosen together.

For $q<3/4$, the LOW cancellation equation $K(1-4q/3)=2q/9$ has solution
$K=2q/(9-12q)$. The HIGH reserve has zero $b$-slope if
$q(\lambda-1)=K(5-6\rho_0)(9/2-\lambda)$.
Solving gives
\[
 K=\frac{67}{48},\qquad \lambda=\frac{16281}{4106},\qquad
 B_0=\frac{1-\rho_0+K(5-6\rho_0)}{q+K(5-6\rho_0)}
     =\frac{2025}{2053},\qquad B=B_0-\epsilon.
\]
These are exact rational choices. Their purpose is to widen the usable source
region while preserving a positive reserve in both branches.

\section{Geometry, exact row certificates and zero exclusion}
The linked geometric parameters are
\begin{align*}
 \theta&=\frac{4374299}{5000000},&
 h&=\frac{3969737987}{5000000000},&
 \ell&=\frac{627103}{3750000},\\
 x&=\frac{5599198039}{15000000000},&
 y&=\frac{6892389961}{15000000000},&
 \kappa_*&=\frac{1874299}{2500000}.
\end{align*}
They satisfy $\ell=11/3-4\theta$, $x=1+\ell-h$, $y=h-2\ell$ and
$\kappa_*=2\theta-1$. Set $d_-=h-1/1000$ and $d_+=h+10^{-10}$.
The new and old capacities are, respectively,
\[
 C_{\rm new}(d)=\frac{2y-(2-B)d}{\lambda},\qquad
 C_{\rm old}(d)=\frac{\min\{d,2y-d\}}{6\kappa_*}.
\]
For $0\le\delta\le3/4$ and $0\le p\le1/2$, write
\[
 V=5/6-\delta,\quad D=3-p-\frac{2p}{3\kappa_*},\quad
 P=2(1-p)\left(1-\frac p{3\kappa_*}\right),\quad J=VD+\delta P>0.
\]
The row-count exponent is $R=1-\delta+\delta PV/(2J)$.
For either capacity $C$, the literal scalar source exponent is
\[
 E(d,C,\delta,p)=\frac{1+\delta}{2}+\frac{13h}{75}-\frac y2-\frac\ell2
 +\ell\delta p+d\left(\frac14+\frac{3R}{4}-\frac{17}{50}\right)
 -\frac{\delta p C}{2}.
\]
Multiplication of $\theta-\nu-E$ by $J>0$ clears the denominator.
With $u=1/2-p$, the resulting polynomial is
$\sum_{i=0}^3u^i(A_i\delta^2+B_i\delta+C_i)$.
For $i=0,1$, exact rational coefficients satisfy $A_i>0$ and
$4A_iC_i-B_i^2>0$, hence
\[
 4A_i(A_i\delta^2+B_i\delta+C_i)
 =(2A_i\delta+B_i)^2+(4A_iC_i-B_i^2)>0.
\]
The remaining coefficient polynomials are nonnegative for $\delta\ge0$.
This global certificate proves the new top saving $10^{-6}$ at $d=h$,
the old junction saving $10^{-5}$ at $d=d_-$, and the old half-row saving
$10^{-3}$ at $d=1/2$. Exact tables and the identities linking them to
$E$ are supplied with the Lean source. Affine interpolation, the positive
junction capacity difference, and the retained $\kappa$-feedback lemmas extend
these estimates to the complete row range $1/200\le d\le d_+$.

The final analytic structure is inherited from Cycle61. If the actual Hecke
zero supremum $\beta$ exceeded $\theta$, its detector parameter
$\kappa=2\beta-1$ would lie in the required interval. The row certificates
and feedback give a strict deficit relative to $\beta$. The physical main and
error families pay the finite auxiliary losses using the reserves above and
produce the same normalized common probe in both branches. The inherited
continuation and zero-exclusion argument then yields $\beta\le\theta$.
The Hecke-to-Dirichlet comparison transports that conclusion to
\eqref{eq:target}. The supplied source contains that complete assembly.
