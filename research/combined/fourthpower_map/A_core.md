## 2. Group overview

**Mathematical step.** The group builds the fourth-power route and its sixth-power comparator.
1. Unit identity: for a sextic χ with χ⁶ = 1 and a unit u, row(k·u⁴) = χ(u)²·row(k). The factor χ(u)² is a cube root of unity.
2. Actual moduli: for fifth-free supported a, sexticGauss a(D⁴k) = χ_a(D)²·sexticGauss a(k) for all D. It vanishes when D shares a prime with a.
3. A family of slots with one coefficient vector becomes one polynomial evaluated at D⁴k.
4. Averaging over D (density 1/W) and pushing (D,k) to D⁴k, with divisor-bounded fibres, gives the T^ε bound in `actual_average`.
5. The sixth route (`SexticSieve`, `SixthDecomposition`) gives exact decomposition with D-dependent masks and root weights N(D)^(−3). Its sextic sieve, `OptimalBound`, is defined but not proved here.
6. `GlobalSlotSelection` picks width at least 43/100 of the total, overshoot at most one mesh. The gate (3/4)·(unselected) < selected holds with reserve S/400.
7. Native chain (13 files): bounds on literal natural slots with exponent width + κ·Σw + ε, κ = max(3/4, 2β − 1), under β ≥ 51/100 and Q ≤ (72).
8. `OlderIncumbent*`: β ≤ 0.87481891 gives 2β − 1 < 3/4, so κ = 3/4.

**Inputs.** Baseline modules (`whole_slot_removal`, zero-slot certificate, reference and capacity energies, PR67 incumbent). Directory modules: `MixedLedger` (slot gate), `KernelCollisionInverse`, `KernelCollisionEnergy`, `KernelNaturalSlots`, `ReferencePrimeEnclosure`, `RestorationFamily`, `RestorationInverseEnergy`, `RestorationCollisionEnergy`, `ReferenceForwardRows`.

**Outputs (importers).** Kernel: `KernelWholeColumns`, `KernelIdeal`, `KernelFormal`, `KernelForwardMass`. Sieve: `SieveOperator`, `SievePrimitive`. Mixed: `MixedFirstNonexceptionalNormalization`, `MixedCenteredEnergy`. OuterFresh: `OuterFreshMixedCapacity`, `OuterFreshSelection`. Restored: `RestoredInitialSource`, `RestoredRootDictionary`. Return: nine files. Final: `FinalFromRawSides`.

## 4. Why the fourth-power transfer saves slot cost (best explanation)

Points 1–4 are read from the proofs. Points 5–6 are inferred from the premise plus the group's costing lemmas.

1. **The fourth power is (inferred) the smallest power that kills all fifth-free non-unit moduli.** For p ∣ D, p⁴ ∣ D⁴, so p^(n+1) ∣ D⁴k for every n < 4 (`prime_power_nonunit_zero`). `FifthFree` says every prime exponent of a is at most 4. So `fifth_free_nonunit_zero` gives exact vanishing on non-coprime D. A cube would not meet the lemma's divisibility requirement for an exponent-4 prime (inferred). The output frequency then grows like N(D)⁴, with `frequency_norm_bound` giving 16KW⁴; the sixth-power analogue would be about 64KW⁶ (inferred).

2. **Phase instead of mask, on the same vanishing pattern.** On coprime D the fourth-power transfer multiplies by χ_a(D)², a unimodular cube root of unity. On non-coprime D it is zero. The sixth-power route has the same zero pattern (`row_sixth_power`: a coprimality mask), with phase 1 (`GaussUnits.shift` with u⁶ and `value_sixth` give exact invariance on units; inferred for Gauss sums). So the two routes differ only by the cubic phase and by the size of the frequency multiplier (inferred).

3. **One coefficient vector, no D-dependent masks.** `whole_column` carries its own contrast: "One common column vector over the entire D-family. No D-dependent coefficient replacement or removal of natural zeros occurs here." The sixth route instead splits the energy by sixth root D (`exact_energy_decomposition`), each with its own coefficients `maskedCoefficient a D`. Each needs, as far as the statements show, a separate application of the sieve (`OptimalBound` requires coefficients common to the row family; inferred) and carries root weight N(D)⁻³ (`central_root_weight`).

4. **Cubic phases are absorbed, not squared away.** The premise says the phase "disappears in mean squares". In the code the phase stays inside the norm: `actual_average` has ‖∑_i b_i·χ_i(D)²·gaussRow_i(k)‖² on the left. `whole_column` rewrites the whole sum over slots as one polynomial value gaussPolynomial(D⁴k). The average is then controlled by the output sum (`weighted_pushforward`), with fibres bounded by divisor counts (`fiber_card`). The only losses left are the density 1/W and the ε-power (16KW⁴)^ε.

5. **No fractional sieve majorant on this route (inferred as a source of the saving).** The classical route passes through `OptimalBound`, with majorant A + B + A^(5/6)B^(1/3) + A^(1/3)B^(5/6). The bound in `actual_average` has no such term. The group does not convert these losses into slot cost.

6. **Costing arithmetic (premise, checked against group A).** With φ = 43/100, the premise's blended cost is 2(1 − φ) + 6φ = 2 + 4φ = 93/25 = 3.72. The classical value is 6κ = 4.5 with κ = 3/4. The saving holds whenever 2 + 4φ < 9/2, that is φ < 5/8. The gate (3/4)(1 − φ) < φ holds exactly when φ > 3/7 (`MixedLedger.slot_gate`). Because 2 + 4φ increases in φ, the infimum under this gate is 26/7 at φ ↓ 3/7, and 93/25 − 26/7 = 1/175. Per residual unit the cost falls from 4.5 to 2. Per selected unit it rises to 6, which is above 4.5. So the saving comes from the residual share (57%) and the selection is the price of the gate (inferred from the premise).
