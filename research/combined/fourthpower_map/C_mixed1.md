# C_mixed1: first third of the Mixed* group

Read-only map of the 48 files in `research/combined/fourthpower_map/lists/C_mixed1.txt`. All are under `src/Candidate/FourthPower/` in the Sil fourth-power submission (`pr74/submissions/sil-fourth-power-20261025`). No Lean file was edited, and neither `lake` nor `lean` was run.

Conventions: "(inferred)" marks my reading, not something stated in the code. "(outside list)" marks a file I read only because my files depend on it. Line numbers are those of the declaration keyword.

**Method.** A scratchpad script printed every file's header and every declaration's signature and docstring (368 declarations in total). Full statements were read for the central declarations. Proofs were read in part for `uniform_centered_source_moment`, `uniform_chosen_first_energy`, `reference_exponent_margins`, `diagonal_slack_pos`, `diagonal_exponent_saved` and `selected_weight`.

## 2. Group overview

These 48 files are the alphabetical first third of the Mixed* group. They build the mixed moment from genuine objects, with no abstract Input source: the MixedActual* files treat the second side (second common labels and physical blocks), and the MixedFirst* files treat the first side (first residual energy, dyadic bands, cutoffs, reference). The development runs in five steps.

1. Labels and families: MixedActualFamily, MixedActualColumns and MixedActualAdmissibility choose one character family per common label and pack ordinary and annular slots into tuples, with explicit caps.
2. Extraction: MixedCommon*, MixedAllocation*, MixedDivisorMask and Mass, MixedArtificialRestriction and MixedDiagonal remove genuine common ideals, split artificial divisors into allocations, and restore the squarefree mask and the fifth-free gates.
3. Centering: MixedCentered*, MixedCanonicalSource and Envelope, and MixedFirstCenteredSourceMoment apply the baseline canonical estimate to the difference of two equal-area rectangles.
4. Components: MixedFirstExceptional*, MixedFirstActualNonexceptional, MixedFirstAvoidance and MixedFirstFourParts split the first energy into zero, nonexceptional, exceptional and discarded parts.
5. Gates and budgets: MixedFirstCutoffs, MixedFirstGeometricCaps (R, sharpSupport, diagonalSlack), MixedFirstChosenEnergy (referenceExponent and the mixed and native gates), MixedFirstFullBudget (four costs) and MixedFirstFullEnergy (final estimate).

Inputs: baseline modules under `Submissions.openai_baseline` (not in this snapshot), and Candidate Mixed modules (Principal*, Supplier*, SelectedVolume, LiteralAllocation, InnerExceptionalLedger, OriginalNormCap). MixedFirstChosenEnergy also imports MixedFirstUniformEnergy and MixedFirstUniformLossBudget.

Outputs (importers, from the directory): MixedFirstChosenEnergy is imported by MixedFirstChosenReferenceSource, MixedFirstReferenceActualCaps, MixedFirstReheight, OuterFreshDeclaredRadius and RestoredRootSource. MixedFirstFullBudget is imported by MixedFirstIntegratedWindows and MixedFirstPaidCosts (outside list), and MixedFirstPaidCosts defines `Parameters`, `Gates` and `SourceGeometry`. MixedFirstCanonicalGeometry is imported by MixedFirstChosenReferenceSource, MixedFirstReferenceDispatch, MixedFirstSeededMoment, OuterFreshSelectedWindow and RestoredRootSource. MixedFirstExceptionalNormalization is imported by MixedFirstExceptionalWindow and MixedFirstGeometricCaps (both in the list), MixedFirstNonexceptionalNormalization, RepairedFirstRawSides and RepairedRawExceptional.

Above this group, outside the list: MixedLedger.lean (exact gate arithmetic, containing 43/100, 3/4 and 1/400), GlobalSlotSelection.lean (greedy 43/100 selection), and OuterFresh* (93/25 root capacity).

### 3b. The terms

- **fourth / fourth power**: the K4 scale 16·K·W⁴ with the outer 1/W (MixedActualGeometry); the exponent 4 on the selected log-volume d in the mixed budget; "fourth-power transfer" (MixedFirstAveragedWindow header); "zero frequencies excluded before fourth powers" (MixedFirstExceptionalWindow header).
- **sixth**: PowerFree 6 and sixthRemainder (MixedDiagonal); "physical sixth-power mask" (MixedExceptionalDictionary.excluded_natural_presentation docstring). The fifth-free gate PowerFree 5 is the main restriction throughout.
- **selected** (three senses in these files):

## 4. The slot-width ledger: best reading

This section is my reading. The formulas are read from the files, and the interpretation is marked.

### 4.1 Quantities (read)

- R = inputWidth η K Z = log_Z K + log_Z N(η) + log_Z c_fixed (MixedFirstGeometricCaps.lean:84).
- P = log_Z s.T (uniform_centered_source_moment, uniform_chosen_first_energy).
- S = Σ over the live subset T of w_i, with 0 ≤ w_i ≤ Lslot. The proof step `hprod : ∏_{i∈T} s.P i = Z^S` (MixedFirstChosenEnergy, inside uniform_chosen_first_energy) identifies S as the total log-width of the slot scales.
- d = log_Z(volume q), where volume q = ∏_{j∈q.J} q.P j (MixedSelectedVolume.lean:23). The proof step `hqpower : volume q = Z^d` confirms this.

### 4.2 Gates (read)

- mixed: P + 2S + 4d ≤ R − ρ (hypothesis of uniform_centered_source_moment, line 314, and of uniform_chosen_first_energy, line 143). The strict form P + 2S + 4d < R is the hypothesis of reference_exponent_choice (line 36); the ρ-form is hcap of reference_exponent_margins (line 49).
- native: (3/4)·S < d (hnative, same statements).
- Reference: referenceExponent (MixedFirstChosenEnergy:32) is 0 in the uncentered branch, and otherwise the midpoint of MixedLedger.lower and MixedLedger.upper (outside list).

### 4.3 Costs 2 and 4, and 6 for the selected part (read; interpretation inferred)

- Read: the coefficient on S is 2, and the extra coefficient on d is 4.
- If the selected width is part of S (inferred: d ⊆ S), then 2S + 4d = 2(S − d) + 6d. So each unit of unselected width costs 2, and each unit of selected width costs 2 + 4 = 6. This matches the brief.
- With d = φ·S, the cost per unit of total width is 2 + 4φ. With φ = 43/100 this is 93/25 = 3.72, the coefficient in the outside root-capacity statements (OuterFreshSelection.lean:190-201).
- Why the 4 (inferred): the selected product is raised to the fourth-power scale (MixedSelectedVolume header; K4 in MixedActualGeometry), so its log-volume is multiplied by 4. This is the fourth-power transfer of the brief.
- Why the 2 (inferred, not derived in these files): the slot products enter the energy quadratically, so each unit of log-width is paid twice. The squares in zeroCost and slotCost are consistent with this, but I did not check the exponent's derivation.
- The classical comparison 6κ = 4.5 does not occur in these files.

### 4.4 The native gate and the 3/7 threshold (read in MixedLedger; interpretation inferred)

- MixedLedger.slot_gate (outside list): if (43/100)·S ≤ d and s ≤ S − d, then (3/4)·s < d. The gate constrains the complement s = S − d, which is the unselected width.
- MixedLedger.slot_reserve (outside): (43/100)·S − (3/4)·(57/100)·S = S/400. So the margin at φ = 43/100 is 1/400.
- GlobalSlotSelection.lean:81-111 (outside) applies the same gate to the complement J\R, whose width is exactly S − d.
- Interpretation (inferred): the 3/4 is κ. nonexceptionalCost (MixedFirstFullBudget.lean:86-91) and liveWeight (MixedAllocationCost.lean:90-91) charge κ per unit of width on the nonexceptional and live parts. The native gate then says that the κ-charged unselected width is dominated by the selected width d.
- Threshold (inferred): (3/4)(1−φ) < φ holds exactly when φ > (3/4)/(7/4) = 3/7, which is κ/(1+κ).

### 4.5 Flag: the formal pair uses one S (read; needs checking)

- In these files the native hypothesis (3/4)·S < d sits next to the mixed budget P + 2S + 4d ≤ R − ρ, with the same S: MixedFirstCenteredSourceMoment.lean:314, MixedFirstChosenEnergy.lean:143, and Gates.mixed and Gates.native in MixedFirstPaidCosts.lean:116-130 (outside list). Gates has both fields on the same p.S.
- Read literally, with d ≤ S, the native inequality (3/4)·S < d requires that the selected width exceed 3/4 of the total. That is incompatible with φ = 43/100.
- The brief's native gate applies to the complement (S − d), as in MixedLedger.slot_gate and GlobalSlotSelection. The budget must use the total S to produce 2 + 4φ.
- If S is the total: the budget gives 2 + 4φ, but the native hypothesis fails at φ = 0.43.
- If S is the complement: the native hypothesis holds, but the budget becomes 2S_u + 4d = (2 + 2φ) per unit of total width, not 2 + 4φ.
- I could not find, in these files, the instantiation that reconciles these. The candidate is chosenParameters and uniform_actual_gates in MixedFirstPaidCosts.lean (lines 663-700, outside list), which I did not examine in detail.

### 4.6 Allocation-side "selected" (read; a separate mechanism)

- In MixedAllocationCost.liveWeight the exponent is κ·Σ over the live slots of w_i, divided by N(∏B).
- In MixedDivisorMask.selected_budget_bound (line 71), Z^{κ·Σ_selectedLive w}/N(∏ selectedDivisor) ≤ Z^{κ·Σ w}/N(D). A slot that carries a prime of D leaves the κ-exponent and adds to the denominator.
- selectedLive is {i : selectedDivisor(inl i) = 1} (MixedLiteralAllocation.lean:96-97, outside list). Its complement carries the prime copies.
- So in the allocation ledger, slots that receive a prime of D cost nothing in κ and save through the denominator. This is not the 2 and 4 accounting of section 4.3, and the two should not be merged.
- The definitions of liveIndices, selectedDivisor and Allocation are in baseline modules, not in this snapshot, so this reading relies on the use sites.
