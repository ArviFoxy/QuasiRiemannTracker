## 4. Best reading: the fourth-power transfer in the mixed moment

Three roles of the fourth power appear in these files. Only the first two are read here. The third is the mechanism I infer.

**(a) Which sums get F^4 (arithmetic, read).** Each column ideal I is fifth-free (`PowerFree 5`), so its valuations are at most 4. `extract_dvd_fourth` then gives: the part of I supported on the primes of F divides F^4. Consequently every extracted label in the artificial-divisor expansion lies in the divisors of F^4 (`boundedLabels`, `bounded_labels_card`). `bounded_fiber_sum` regroups the tuple sum over these labels. The label count is a subpower, <= K * N(F)^eps (`bounded_labels_subpower`). This is an exact covering, not an estimate.

**(b) Which analytic sums get 16*K*W^4 (read).** A radius K is replaced by 16 K W^4: as the ball-profile argument in the inner exceptional block (`uniform_actual_block`), as the Kphys argument of the physical-cost ledger (`savedBudget_exact`), in the physical blocks (`pushCost`, `uniform_integrated_windows`), and in the nonexceptional suppliers (`uniform_nonexceptional_subsets`). `pushCost` takes two distinct radii KL and KR for the two fourth-power transfers ("retain their distinct actual radii"). Which of the two first children (`first_gauss_window`, `first_gauss_conjugate_window`) each radius belongs to is inferred, not read. The cost is explicit: `fourth_kernel_cost` gives (16KW^4)^(5/6)/W = 16^(5/6) K^(5/6) W^(7/3), and `pushCost_upper` gives pushCost <= mass * Cpush * 16^eps * Z^(5B eps), because K, W <= Z^B.

**(c) Mechanism (inferred).** The Lean identity behind (b) is outside this list: `GaussTransfer.fourth_power` (outside) states that for fifth-free a, sexticGauss a (D^4 k) = (idealRowHom D)^2 * sexticGauss a k. Its `normalized_fourth_power` gives the same for normalized Gauss rows. When D is coprime to a, the factor (idealRowHom D)^2 is a cube root of unity, since (idealRowHom D)^6 = 1 (the h6 step in its proof); when D is not coprime to a, both sides vanish. The fourth-power transfer therefore multiplies by a unimodular cubic phase and moves the argument to D^4 k, whose norm is about N(D)^4, which is about W^4 (inferred).

**(d) How the cubic phase is removed (read for the phase bounds, inferred for the mean-square step).** In these files every phase is unimodular: `selectedTwist_norm` (<= 1), `selected_coefficient_norm` (|selectedCoefficient| <= |sourceCoefficient|), `frozen_phase_norm`, `selected_row_hom_norm`, `commonPhase_norm`. The quantities that matter are squared norms or energies. `selected_energy_allocation` gives ||staticFrozen||^2 * selectedEnergy(child), and `rowVector_pair` puts sqrt(w(F)/W) on each side of Cauchy. So the phase drops out of every mean square. `cubic_selected_row` absorbs the cubic character of the selected divisor into the row parameter (twist by f equals the original character at f^2 z), which is why eligibility is tested at F_gen^2 * z (`selected_character_nonprincipal`).

**(e) The sixth power, for contrast (read).** m^6 appears in the row weight (`weight_height`). The sixth-power mask is kept as a mask: `selected_energy_mask` shows that multiplying the mask by the R-puncture (R divides s.R) leaves the selected energy unchanged.

Which divisor carries the fourth power, F (selected) or D (seed), is not fixed by these files. In the seed allocation the reciprocal of the full norm is kept (`seed_frozen_cost`: N(seed)^(-1), not N(seed)^(-2/3)).

## 5. Discrepancies and checks for the parent

1. **The mixed gate here gives 4 per selected unit, not 6.** Every mixed gate in these files reads P + 2S + 4d (`MixedLedger.reference_gate`, `seeded_mixed_gates`, `chosenReference`, `uniform_actual_gates`, the seven `MixedFirstOuterUniformity` hypotheses). The native gate in the same files is (3/4)S < d with the same S (`MixedFirstPaidCosts.lean:698`, `MixedFirstOuterUniformity` lines 105, 224, ...). `MixedLedger.slot_gate` puts the native gate on s = S - d, the unselected width, and `GlobalSlotSelection.large_branch` (outside) has the same form: (3/4) times the sum over J minus R, less than the sum over R. So the S in the native gate of these files must be the unselected width. Reading S as unselected, the gate charges 2 per unselected unit and 4 per selected unit. That gives 2(1-phi) + 4phi = 2 + 2phi per unit of total width, not 2 + 4phi. The assignment's 93/25 = 2 + 4phi (selected costs 6) therefore needs an extra 2d somewhere, for example in P = logb(T/pairNorm d) or in the total-width term. I could not find it in these files. Check `MixedFirstSourceGeometry` (where T is defined) and the outer files.
2. **3/7 and 93/25 are not literal here.** The threshold 3/7 is only derived (section 3). 93/25 appears only in outside files.
3. **43/100 and 1/400 check out.** (43/100) - (3/4)(57/100) = 172/400 - 171/400 = 1/400, and (3/4)(57/100) = 0.4275 < 0.43 = 43/100, so the selection gate holds with margin 1/400 (`MixedLedger.slot_reserve`, `slot_gate`).
4. **Declarations not fully read.** Statements truncated in the extract (for example the right-hand sides of `uniform_chosen_outer_energy`, `uniform_integrated_windows` and `actual_common_energy`) are described from their docstrings and the visible parts. Their full statements were not read.
