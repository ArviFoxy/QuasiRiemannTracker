3. **One explicit family of faces instead of a finite source graph.** In the continuum limit the source step is the ODE `E' = −6q*E/(1 − 6q*s)` for the envelope of the faces, with the binding threshold `6q* = 5(√E − 2√s)²/(2 − E − 4√(Es))` over amplitude classes (each single-class flow is solved by the Lambert W function). The faces are the secant lines of a five-piece cubic envelope that follows this flow, 2^16 faces per piece. Each piece is certified once for all its faces: the scalar gates by one-variable polynomial positivity certificates in the face parameter, the new HIGH gate by Bernstein trees in density and amplitude with polynomial coefficients (`Faceless/`). The chain is an induction along each piece, joined by four concrete junction steps. 4. **Global physical face in the final return.** The physical face is applied through its global source theorem at every shift (Lemma D), so the windowed physical schedule is no longer needed. The final return is re-certified with the last face of the family as count face and an interior face as physical face (34 row rectangles, 11 admission windows).
