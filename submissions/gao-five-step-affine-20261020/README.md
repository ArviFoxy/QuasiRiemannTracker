# Five affine steps with joint returned-source counting

The proof establishes the all-moduli Dirichlet boundary
`8748087/10000000 = 0.8748087`, with the principal pole excluded.

[Four-page paper](paper/paper.pdf) · [Paper source](paper/paper.tex) ·
[Canonical theorem](src/Candidate/Main.lean)

The construction keeps LOW continuations and their reciprocal weights through
five finite affine stages. Returned HIGH records retain their arithmetic
prefixes until a joint density payment. Constants, mesh choices and height
thresholds are selected before the moving source. The final detector and
weighted numerator use the same constructed root moment.

`Candidate.bound` has no additional analytic hypothesis. Its necessary local
Lean check reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
Official Comparator, independent-kernel replay and tracker admission are
handled by the repository’s submission workflow.

The submission imports the pinned OpenAI baseline and Akash Levy’s reusable
weighted-numerator modules. Its reached native affine source is retained from
ArviFoxy’s PR 67, including Hailey Collet’s and earlier contributors’ notices.
The new finite-depth construction is under `Candidate/Research/RecursiveAffine/`.
