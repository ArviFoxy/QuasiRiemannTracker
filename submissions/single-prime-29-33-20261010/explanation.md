# Single compensating-prime bound

The exact bound is `29/33`; a truncated decimal approximation is `0.87878787`.
`Candidate.bound` states the canonical all-Dirichlet nonvanishing theorem,
including every positive modulus and the principal-character pole exception.
It applies the endpoint theorem in the original ZetaZeroFree development.

This submission contains the 35 rebuilt proof modules and a standard entrypoint.
Their module paths begin with `Candidate`; import paths are updated accordingly.
The original theorem bodies and namespaces are preserved. The immutable source
package, manuscripts and earlier evidence remain under
`proofs/single-prime-29-33-20261010/`.

The maintainer supplies the pinned OpenAI and Mathlib libraries. The complete
standard challenge is generated independently from `manifest.json`, then
Comparator and Lean, NanoDa and con-ron check its exported proof closure.
The checked target is the canonical all-Dirichlet declaration.

See `NOTICE` for the original authorship and source provenance. The source
foundation is OpenAI's pinned intermediate development. Its bibliography is in
`references`; the standard wrapper applies this contribution's endpoint.
